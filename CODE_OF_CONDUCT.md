# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

RADAR's project spaces should support respectful, evidence-led technical discussion. Participants are expected to:

- be considerate and precise;
- critique claims, code, methods, and evidence rather than people;
- acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes openly;
- respect differing experience levels, identities, and viewpoints;
- protect private or sensitive information; and
- avoid harassment, intimidation, discrimination, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, and deliberate disruption.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, reviews, community conversations, demonstrations, and other spaces where a person is representing RADAR.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact method on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public discussion.

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content, restrict participation, or exclude a participant from project spaces. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially and impartially as practical. Retaliation against a reporter or witness is not acceptable.
